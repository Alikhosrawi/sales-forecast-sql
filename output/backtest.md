# Backtest

Forecast as of week 6 of each of the two quarters before the current one, compared with the closed-won total that quarter finished with. Week 6 is the 6th Monday snapshot inside the quarter. The forecast is the same weighted number this pipeline would have produced that week. Error = forecast − actual. A positive error means the week-6 forecast was higher than the result.
